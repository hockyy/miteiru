# What's New in Miteiru 8.3.0

Changes since [v8.2.0](https://github.com/hockyy/miteiru/releases/tag/v8.2.0).

## Japanese readings that fit the line
Furigana now follows the reading a word has in its sentence, not just its most common one.

- **More readings come out right.** On 65 lines we wrote as hard cases (日本酒, 米から, 今日, 一日, 人気, 上手, counters and names), 94.6% of kanji words now get the right reading, up from 84.9%. On the JVS speech corpus, wrong kana fell from 1.10% to 0.78%.
- **Counters take their sound changes.** 一本 is いっぽん, 三本 さんぼん, 一人 ひとり, 三日 みっか, 四月一日 ついたち, 二十歳 はたち.
- **Numbers in kanji stay one word.** 二十, 三百 and 二〇二六 are read whole.
- **Context words.** 行った is おこなった only after an object with を, and 何 is なん before の, と, だ, です and counters.
- **Honorifics stay whole.** お送りした, お話しした and お待ちいただけます are one word each.
- The word costs behind this come from the [pyopenjtalk-plus](https://github.com/tsukumijima/pyopenjtalk-plus) dictionary (BSD and MIT).

## Word hints that fit how a word is used
- **Japanese.** The hint follows the word's part of speech: さん is the name suffix, not "acid", and と is "with", "and" or "if" depending on where it stands.
- **Mandarin and Cantonese.** Long meanings are no longer dropped, so 没想到, 玉皇大帝, 龙王, 飲茶 and 咗 have hints again. A surname no longer wins over the common word (后 is "back").

## Mandarin and Cantonese
- **Pinyin is read for the whole line.** 了 at the end of a sentence is le, not liǎo.
- **Traditional characters read right.** 我們 is wǒ men and 什麼 is shén me; they used to come out as mén and mó.
- **Fixed: the tone setting.** "Tone numbers" showed contour marks for Mandarin, and Cantonese had the two settings swapped. Numbers now show ni3 and nei5; marks show nǐ and Cantonese's contour marks.

## AI assistants
Miteiru can now work with AI assistants that speak MCP, such as Claude Desktop, Claude Code or Codex.

- **Set it up** in **Settings → AI → AI assistants**, which shows the command to add, with this computer's path.
- **What an assistant can do:**
  - Find YouTube videos with captions in your language.
  - Rate how much of a video you'd understand from the words you've saved.
  - Open a video in Miteiru with its subtitles set up.
  - Read text the way Miteiru does: words, readings, dictionary meanings, JLPT or HSK levels, word frequency, pitch accent and tones, conjugation tables and kanji or hanzi stroke order.
  - Mark words as known or learning. My words and the subtitle colours change at once.
- **Safety.** Only programs on this computer can connect, and web pages are refused. YouTube searches are kept small so your computer isn't flagged as a bot, and assistants are told to treat video text as content, never as instructions.
- The analyzer's Python command-line tool is gone; the assistant tools replace it.

## For developers
- A checkout of Miteiru can run the assistant tools without the app: `node scripts/miteiru-mcp.mjs`. It runs the same Rust tokenizers as the app. See [docs/LOCAL_API.md](https://github.com/hockyy/miteiru/blob/main/docs/LOCAL_API.md).
- Japanese words have JLPT levels (Jonathan Waller's lists, via [yomitan-jlpt-vocab](https://github.com/stephenmk/yomitan-jlpt-vocab), CC BY-SA 4.0) and frequencies ([wordfreq](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq), CC BY-SA 4.0).

## Downloads
- **Windows:** `Miteiru_8.3.0_x64-setup.exe`. Install it over 8.2.0; your words, settings and downloaded languages stay.
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Miteiru_8.3.0_aarch64.dmg`. Still not notarized: [open it anyway](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/open-a-mac-app-from-an-unidentified-developer-mh40616/mac).
- **Linux:** `Miteiru_8.3.0_amd64.deb` and `.AppImage`, built on Ubuntu 22.04. Video plays through the system's libmpv: the `.deb` installs it; for the AppImage, install `libmpv2` (`libmpv1` on Ubuntu 22.04).
- **Language packs:** `Miteiru-language-*.mtrpack`. Miteiru downloads these itself; they're here for installing without a connection.
- **Updating:** 8.3 doesn't update itself. New versions appear on the [releases page](https://github.com/hockyy/miteiru/releases) and at [miteiru.hocky.id](https://miteiru.hocky.id).
