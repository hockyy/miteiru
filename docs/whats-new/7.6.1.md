# What's New in Miteiru 7.6.1

Changes since [v7.6.0](https://github.com/hockyy/miteiru/releases/tag/v7.6.0).

## Features
- **A new seek bar.**
  - It sits inside one panel with the player controls, with no gap above them, and lines up with the buttons underneath.
  - It has a rounded track and a blue progress bar, and its thumb glows when you hover or drag.
- **Subtitle lines on the seek bar.** The primary subtitle's lines are marked on the track, so you can see where people talk. Hovering shows the time and the line spoken there.
- The volume, speed and video-settings sliders match the new look: blue up to the value.

## Fixes
- Vietnamese words keep their spaces. With "Space between words" off (the default), lines read "Hôm naytrời đẹp" instead of "Hôm nay trời đẹp". This also affected karaoke lyrics, note examples and the ruby text copied for Anki.
- The "1.0×" and "Speed" labels in the player sat a little low; they are centred now.
- A seek-bar drag that is cancelled returns the bar to where the video actually is.
