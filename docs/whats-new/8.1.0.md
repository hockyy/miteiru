# What's New in Miteiru 8.1.0

Changes since [v8.0.1](https://github.com/hockyy/miteiru/releases/tag/v8.0.1).

## AI study tools while you watch
New in this release, all off until you turn them on in **Video settings → AI**, under **While you watch**. They use your [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/keys) key.

- **Box tips.** When the line on screen has a grammar point worth learning, wordplay, a joke, an idiom or a saying, a short card explains it in one sentence. Red marks on the seek bar show where the tips are. A tip stays while you pause, and × hides it.
- **AI word splitting** (Japanese).
  - AI checks where each word begins and ends and adds its meaning in this line.
  - Words your dictionaries don't have are saved to **Your dictionary** and used in later videos too. The word popup marks them **Added by AI**.
  - For Chinese, Cantonese and Vietnamese, it adds missing words to Your dictionary.
- **Your dictionary** (Video settings → AI → Open your dictionary). Add, edit, search and remove words. Each is labelled AI or You.
- **Looks ahead.**
  - Each request covers 30 seconds or 1 minute of the video.
  - Finished parts are kept, so going back costs nothing.
  - If an answer can't be used, the tools pause and say so, with **Retry**. Nothing retries (and charges) by itself.
- **Subtitles from the audio** (YouTube, **Subtitles… → Subtitles from the audio…**). Miteiru transcribes the audio just ahead of you in 30-second paid pieces and adds the lines with readings and meanings.
- **Spending.** Video settings → AI shows what AI has cost on this device, and the top bar shows it while an AI tool is on.

## Faster, clearer AI
- **Study a text:**
  - Answers appear as they are written.
  - A new question cancels the one still running.
  - Asking again reuses the earlier answer.
- **No thinking by default.** AI tools answer without thinking first, which is faster and cheaper. **Let the model think before answering** in Video settings → AI turns it on for models that need it.
- **Subtitle translation** runs three batches at a time, and each batch's English lands on its own lines.
- **Readable errors.** A failed request says why (a wrong key, for example) instead of "could not parse".

## Watching
- **Seek bar.** Dragging it shows the subtitle at the dragged time while the picture follows.
- **Subtitle words line up** when only some of them show a meaning or romaji.
- **Audio track…** (in **Subtitles…**) switches audio while the video plays, for videos with several tracks.
- **Fixes:**
  - **←** near the start of a video no longer jumps to the end.
  - The hearing-impaired cleanup removes only a speaker label at the start of a line. It used to cut text before any colon, so "10:30" became "30".
  - **Next** and **Previous** pick the matching subtitles: `ep01.ja.srt`, then `ep02.ja.srt`.
  - A subtitle that finishes loading after you switch videos no longer lands on the new one.
- **YouTube:**
  - Captions load in the background while the video starts; choose them from the message or from **Subtitles…**.
  - Every link form plays, including `/live/` links, music.youtube.com and links without `https://`.
  - Auto-generated captions no longer show every line twice.
- **Right-click** shows no browser menu over the video. Text fields keep Paste and Copy.
- **Missing libmpv.** When the video player library is missing, Miteiru says so, with the Linux package names, instead of blaming the file.

## Words and study
- **My words** has a search box (word or meaning) and level chips that filter. It opens at once even with 30,000 words, and keeps your place when words change above you.
- **The word popup** shows the clicked word's own reading (いる no longer shows as 居る read おる), and saved levels load for every word.
- **Flashcards** open faster, and say to choose a language when none is loaded.
- **Study a text:**
  - A single line shows in the Sentences list.
  - Failed translations say so, and nothing is sent twice while waiting.

## Reliability
- **Language downloads.** A download in progress stays visible after a reload, and Home says correctly when a language is ready.
- **Helpers stop with Miteiru.** Live captions and other helper programs stop when Miteiru closes or reloads, even after a crash.
- **Errors.** When something breaks, Miteiru shows what happened with **Copy details** and **Reload Miteiru**, instead of a blank "Application error".
- **macOS:** Command+R reloads again, and the Command shortcuts for translation timing work.

## Downloads
- **Windows:** `Miteiru_8.1.0_x64-setup.exe`. Install it over 8.0.1; your words, settings and downloaded languages stay.
- **macOS (Apple silicon):** `Miteiru_8.1.0_aarch64.dmg`. Still not notarized: [open it anyway](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/open-a-mac-app-from-an-unidentified-developer-mh40616/mac).
- **Linux:** `Miteiru_8.1.0_amd64.deb` and `.AppImage`, built on Ubuntu 22.04. Video plays through the system's libmpv: the `.deb` installs it; for the AppImage, install `libmpv2` (`libmpv1` on Ubuntu 22.04).
- **Language packs:** `Miteiru-language-*.mtrpack`. Miteiru downloads these itself; they're here for installing without a connection.
- **Updating:** 8.1 doesn't update itself. New versions appear on the [releases page](https://github.com/hockyy/miteiru/releases) and at [miteiru.hocky.id](https://miteiru.hocky.id).
